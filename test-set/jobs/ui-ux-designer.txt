As a UI/UX Designer at Vertex IT Solutions, you will play a key role in designing intuitive, visually appealing,
and user-centred digital experiences for our web and mobile applications. You will be responsible for
creating wireframes, prototypes, and final design assets that align with business goals and user needs.
This position requires a creative and detail-oriented professional who is passionate about user experience,
design systems, and interface aesthetics. You will collaborate closely with developers, product managers,
and stakeholders to transform ideas into engaging, functional, and visually compelling interfaces that
reflect Vertex’s commitment to quality and innovation.

Key Responsibilities
1. User Experience Design
• Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing to understand user needs and pain points.
• Develop user personas, user journeys, and flow diagrams to guide the design process.
• Translate complex requirements into intuitive, easy-to-navigate user experiences.
2. User Interface Design
• Design clean, modern, and consistent UI layouts for web and mobile applications.
• Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups using tools like Figma, Adobe XD, or
Sketch.
• Maintain and enhance design systems, ensuring visual and interaction consistency across all
products.
• Work closely with developers to ensure seamless handoff and implementation of designs.
3. Collaboration & Communication
• Collaborate with product managers and engineers to align design with business and technical
requirements.
• Present and justify design decisions to stakeholders with data-driven insights.
• Participate in agile ceremonies such as sprint planning and reviews to ensure timely delivery of
design assets.
4. Quality & Continuous Improvement
• Stay updated with the latest UI/UX trends, tools, and technologies.
• Continuously iterate on designs based on user feedback and analytics.
• Contribute to improving overall user experience through innovation and best practices.

Required Skills & Qualifications
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Computer
Science, or a related field.
• 3–5 years of experience as a UI/UX Designer in a software or tech company.
• Proficiency in design and prototyping tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, or InVision.
• Strong understanding of user-centered design principles, responsive design, and accessibility
standards.
• Experience collaborating with front-end developers and product teams.
• Ability to present and explain design concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical
audiences.
• Portfolio showcasing strong UI/UX design skills and real-world project experience.

What We Offer


• Competitive salary package with performance-based incentives.
• A creative, collaborative, and innovation-driven work culture.
• Opportunities for professional growth and continuous learning.
• Exposure to international projects and a global work environment.
• Medical, paid leaves, and other employee benefits.
• The opportunity to make a meaningful impact by designing user experiences that empower and
engage.
